Our Mission

At Union High School, we believe that STEM is for everyone. Our mission is to make complex concepts accessible and exciting for young learners before they even reach middle school. By working directly with local elementary classrooms, we aren't just teaching lessons, we’re showing students that science is something they can do, not just something they read about in a textbook. We also host sessions, outside of our elementary schools, at local businesses and events.
